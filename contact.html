Frequently Asked Questions

Standard domestic refill bookings placed before 6:00 PM are delivered on the same day, while Priority Express bookings reach your doorstep within 2 hours across all covered NCR zones. You receive real-time SMS and consumer portal dispatch tracking once a delivery technician leaves our depot.
Yes. Every BlueFlame delivery van carries a calibrated digital hanging scale. Our technician is required to weigh the sealed cylinder in your presence (gross weight = cylinder tare weight stamped on the stay plate + 14.2 kg or 19 kg net LPG) and check the intact tamper-evident cap seal before completing delivery.
For a new domestic connection, you need a valid Proof of Identity (Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport) and Proof of Residence (utility bill, rent agreement, or Aadhaar). For connection transfers within or outside New Delhi, simply bring or upload your Subscription Voucher (SV) and e-KYC ID via our desk or Consumer Login portal.
Yes. We supply 19 kg and 47.5 kg commercial LPG cylinders as well as multi-cylinder VOT/LOT cascade installations for restaurants, cloud kitchens, bakeries, hospitals, and industrial facilities with consolidated monthly GST tax invoices and priority route scheduling.
If you smell gas or suspect a leak, immediately close the regulator knob, open doors and windows, avoid all switches or flames, and call our 24/7 Emergency Helpline at 1906 or +91 1800-233-3555. For routine 2-year safety audits or Suraksha rubber hose replacements, call our support desk or book a visit on the Services page.
