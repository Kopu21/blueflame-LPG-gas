The BlueFlame 5-Point Delivery Promise

We don't just drop off gas cylinders. Every single delivery adheres to our strict safety, weight, and timing charter to safeguard your family and business.

1. Verified Doorstep Weight Check

Our delivery personnel carry certified digital hanging scales. You can demand a live pre-delivery weight check before accepting the cylinder to ensure full 14.2 kg net gas.

Net Gas Weight: 14.2 kg Tolerance: ±50 gm

2. Tamper-Proof Hologram Seal

Every cylinder cap is protected by high-security heat-shrink holographic film with a unique QR code. Broken seal? We replace the cylinder immediately at zero cost.

100% Genuine Bottling QR Verified

3. 2-Hour Express Delivery

Ran out of gas while cooking dinner? Select our Express Delivery slot. Our eco-friendly EV delivery fleet navigates local streets to arrive within 120 minutes.

On-Time Or ₹50 Credit Fast Dispatch

4. Free O-Ring & Leak Test

Our trained delivery agent checks the rubber O-ring inside the cylinder valve and performs a soap solution bubble test to certify zero gas leakage before departure.

Free Safety Check Zero Leakage

5. Direct Subsidy & Instant Tax Bill

Govt DBTL direct benefit subsidy is instantly triggered to your bank account upon delivery. Digital GST tax invoice sent directly to your phone & downloadable anytime.

Direct Bank Transfer Instant GST Bill
