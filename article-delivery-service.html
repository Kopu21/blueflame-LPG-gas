LPG Cylinder Doorstep Delivery & Customer Care: Pre-Dispatch Checks & Smooth Handover

RK
Rajesh K. Patel
Senior Logistics Operations Head
Consumer Delivery Rights
  • Mandatory Pre-Delivery Inspection: Every BlueFlame delivery pilot carries calibrated portable spring weighing scales and a soap-water leak tester.
  • Tare Weight Verification: Net gas weight must equal exactly 14.2 kg (total weight = gross weight on collar minus empty cylinder tare weight).
  • Seal & Valve Integrity: Cylinders must feature an intact holographic safety seal and undamaged internal O-ring gasket before handover.
  • Digital OTP & Invoicing: Deliveries are confirmed via secure OTP on the customer's registered phone, immediately generating an authorized digital GST receipt.

1. The Journey from Bottling Plant to Your Doorstep

A seamless LPG delivery begins at the automated bottling terminal where cylinders are filled by electronic carousels, inspected by optical scanners, and sealed with tamper-evident shrink-wrap. At our localized urban distribution depots, cylinders undergo secondary quality assurance: check-weighing on certified scales, hydrostatic expiration date monitoring, and leak submersion baths.

2. What to Check When Your Cylinder Arrives

Consumers hold full statutory rights to verify the safety and exact quantity of their LPG cylinder prior to accepting handover: Request doorstep weight verification using the delivery personnel's portable digital scale (tolerance within ±150 grams). Inspect the holographic safety seal around the brass valve to ensure it is unbroken. Request free installation and a soap bubble check to verify an airtight seal before the pilot leaves.

3. Digital Customer Care & Emergency Support

Our agency coordinates customer support via automated WhatsApp refill bots, IVRS phone lines, and bilingual customer care specialists. In the rare event of a reported gas smell, our specialized emergency patrol vehicles reach customer residences within 2 hours equipped with gas-sniffing detectors and spare safety hardware.
