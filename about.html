Zero-Tolerance Safety

Our Uncompromising Safety Commitment

Operating an authorized LPG distribution network requires unyielding adherence to national petroleum safety codes. We prioritize consumer safety at every touchpoint.

Read Safety Guidelines

Statutory Hydrostatic Testing

No cylinder leaves our depot without verified statutory test stamps. Cylinders reaching their 5-year re-test cycle are retired for factory hydrostatic pressure testing.

O-Ring Gasket Replacement

The internal rubber O-ring is the primary barrier preventing leakage at the regulator neck. Any worn or hardened gasket is replaced immediately with PESO-certified nitrile rubber.

Consumer Safety Education

We proactively educate families on the safe soap bubble leak test, kitchen ventilation rules, rubber hose replacement intervals, and overnight regulator shut-off practices.
